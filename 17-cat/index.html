Point. Drag. — × — Done.

Shortty is a free screenshot tool for macOS. Grab any part of the screen, mark it up without flattening a thing, and send it on its way.

Capture

Ten ways to grab the screen.

Everything lives in the menu bar, and every action can have its own global shortcut. You pick them; none are set out of the box.

Region
Drag over an area. Space switches to clicking a window.
Repeat last region
The same area as last time, remembered across relaunches.
Screen
The display under the pointer.
Window
Click a window to capture it, without its shadow.
Active window
The front window of the app you are in, instantly.
Scrolling
Scroll a page and the frames are stitched into one tall image.
Text
The text or QR code in an area goes straight to the clipboard.
Lazy region
Pick the area, then a countdown runs before the shot.
Lazy screen
A countdown, then the display under the pointer.
Reopen last
Brings back the latest capture with its edits, even after a relaunch.
Edit

Nothing gets flattened.

Every arrow, label and blur stays an object. Select it later to move it, resize it or change its color. Hold ⌘ in the editor to see each key.

  • V

    Select

    Move, resize, recolor anything you drew.

  • P

    Pencil

    Freehand. Shift for a straight line.

  • A

    Arrow

    Four styles, curve it from the middle.

  • T

    Text

    Callout, label or plain letters.

  • RO

    Shapes

    Rectangle or ellipse. Shift makes it a square or a circle.

  • N

    Steps

    1, 2, 3 badges that renumber themselves.

  • M

    Magnifier

    A 2× copy of a small area, joined by a line.

  • B

    Blur

    Blur or erase an area, or only its text.

  • S

    Spotlight

    Dims everything else.

  • X

    Recognize text

    Live Text: select exactly the words you want.

  • K

    Text Card

    Code or a chat, redrawn as a clean card.

  • C

    Crop

    Undo brings the whole picture back.

  • U

    Ruler

    Measure to the nearest edges, in pixels.

And also

The small things that add up.

  • Text Card

    Drag over code, a terminal or a chat and it is redrawn as a window-like card, every word in the color it had. Syntax highlighting survives.

  • Ruler

    Point at anything to measure it. Lines run to the nearest edges with their length in pixels; hold 1 or 2 to measure one way.

  • Quick card

    Skip the editor when you want: a small card in the corner offers Copy, Save, Pin and Edit, then goes away on its own.

  • Recent captures

    The last 20 captures keep their edits, even after a relaunch. Click one to keep working on it.

  • Drag and drop

    Drag the finished picture into any app or upload box. Nothing piles up in Downloads.

  • Pin to screen

    Float a capture above every window while you work from it.

  • Gradient backdrop

    Put the result on a gradient card with padding and a soft shadow, ready to post.

  • Room to work

    Draw or type past the edges of the screenshot and the canvas grows to fit.

  • Private

    Text is recognized on your Mac with Apple Vision and Live Text. Nothing leaves it.

Install

Get Shortty.

Shortty is free, so it isn’t signed with a paid Apple Developer ID ($99 a year). macOS asks you to confirm it once. That’s the only catch.

Download the app

  1. Download the latest Shortty-X.Y.Z.dmg from GitHub Releases.
  2. Open it and drag Shortty onto Applications.
  3. Open Shortty. macOS says it can’t verify the developer: close that message. Don’t move it to the Trash.
  4. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to Security and click Open Anyway next to “Shortty was blocked”. Confirm with Touch ID or your password.
  5. Take a capture. When macOS asks for Screen Recording, allow Shortty and reopen it if asked.
  6. Set your shortcuts in Settings → Shortcuts.

Or skip step 4 from the terminal:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Shortty.app

To update, drag the new version over the old one. Screen Recording stays allowed.

Or build it yourself

No Apple account and no warning: apps you build yourself aren’t flagged as downloaded. You need Xcode (free) and xcodegen.

brew install xcodegen
git clone https://github.com/juan-alvarz/Shortty.git
cd Shortty
Scripts/install.sh

To update later:

git pull && Scripts/install.sh
